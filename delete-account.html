Delete your Gist account

Last updated: September 13, 2026

You can delete your Gist account from inside the app, at any time, without contacting us. Deleting is immediate and cannot be undone.

How to delete it in the app

  1. Open Gist and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You are signed out straight away and the account is gone.

If you cannot sign in and so cannot reach Settings, email totallynotwoojin@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We action it within 30 days, usually within two business days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your email address, display name, profile photo, status and languageDeleted. The profile is anonymised to “Deleted account”.
Your @usernameReleased immediately and can be registered again, including by you.
Your contacts, connections and blocksDeleted.
Your devices, sessions and notification tokensDeleted. No further notifications are sent.
Your membership of every chat and groupDeactivated. You leave every conversation.
Messages and files you already sent to other peopleKept in their chats, shown as from “Deleted account”. They are the other person’s copy of a conversation, so we do not remove them. Delete a message for everyone before deleting your account if you do not want it to remain.

A re-registered username never inherits the old account’s history, contacts or chats.

Retention

Deletion takes effect at once. Encrypted backups of the database are kept for up to 30 days for disaster recovery and then expire, so a copy of deleted rows can persist in those backups for that period. Backups are never used to restore an individual deleted account.
